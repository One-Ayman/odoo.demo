### 8. GL detail: OFFSET-1000 vs keyset-equivalent page (same depth) ###
=== Keyset (cursor) pagination vs OFFSET pagination, General Ledger detail ===
=== account_id=6 (Account Receivable), company_id=1, 5,100,068 total account_move_line rows ===
=== Both variants fetch 21 rows (page_size=20 + 1 lookahead), warm cache ===

Depth        | OFFSET pagination | Keyset pagination | Speedup
row 1,001    | 431.2 ms           | 24.9 ms            | ~17x
row 100,000  | 625.2 ms           | 27.9 ms            | ~22x

Keyset pagination cost stays flat regardless of how deep the page is
(bounded by an index range scan starting at the cursor); OFFSET pagination
must walk and discard every prior row every time, so its cost grows with
depth. At 5.1M rows this is already a meaningful, measured difference; the
gap widens further as the table grows (extrapolating: at 75M rows, an
OFFSET-100000 style deep page would need to walk proportionally more rows,
while keyset pagination's cost is architecturally independent of table
size for a given page).
